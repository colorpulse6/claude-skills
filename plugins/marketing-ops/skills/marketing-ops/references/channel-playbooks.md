## Universal rules

1. **Value-first ratio:** ≥80% of activity on any channel must be useful with the product removed. Channels smell pitch-only accounts instantly and throttle them.
2. **Platform-native or nothing.** A carousel is not a tweet; a tweet is not a LinkedIn post. Reformat, don't cross-post.
3. **One CTA per asset**, matched to funnel stage: cold audience → freebie/follow; warm → product page; hot → purchase.
4. **The 24-hour reply sweep** is where the actual growth is — every comment answered within a day, genuinely.
5. **Faceless ≠ anonymous-spammy.** Consistent brand voice, real expertise in answers, named brand accounts. Faceless removes the person, not the accountability.

## Reddit

The highest-intent channel for problem-aware audiences, and the fastest to ban you.

- **Read each subreddit's rules before the first post** — self-promo policies range from "flair it" to "instant ban." Assume strict until read.
- **The play:** answer real questions in comment sections with complete, generous answers. The product appears only where rules allow: profile bio always; a "tools I use" mention when directly relevant AND permitted; dedicated promo threads where they exist.
- **Account hygiene:** an account that only ever mentions one product is a billboard. Maintain genuine participation ratio ~10:1.
- **Post-shaped plays that work:** "I analyzed N X and found Y" (data posts, receipt included), honest build-logs in r/SideProject-type subs, asking for feedback (and meaning it).
- **Never:** vote manipulation, sockpuppet questions you answer yourself, DM-spamming commenters.

## Product Hunt

A launch is an event you schedule, not a post you fire.

- **Timing:** Tuesday–Thursday, list at 00:01 Pacific — full 24h of voting window.
- **Prep (the week before):** gallery assets (first image carries the pitch), a 40-char tagline that says what it does, a maker's first-comment written in advance (the honest story: what, why, what's next, a question for the community), tag-team availability for all-day replies.
- **The first comment matters more than the listing.** Questions in it drive comment velocity, which drives ranking.
- **Don't** beg for upvotes anywhere public (against rules, and visible); **do** tell your existing list/followers you launched and let them decide.
- Launch day is a reply marathon: every comment answered, feature requests logged publicly.

## Hacker News

- **Show HN** only when there's something to actually try, and the write-up is engineer-honest: what it does, how it works, what's hard, what's broken. Marketing language is detected and punished in the first three comments.
- Respond to criticism technically and without defensiveness — the thread IS the marketing.
- A failed Show HN costs nothing; a defensive one costs reputation. Post, engage honestly, move on.

## LinkedIn

- Works for career/job-search and B2B products; the audience is there in work-mode.
- **Format that carries:** personal-adjacent insight + concrete numbers + line breaks for scannability. Document-style carousels (PDF upload) get outsized reach.
- Native uploads outperform links; put the link in the first comment or use a plain-text CTA ("link in comments").
- Cadence beats bursts: 2–3 posts/week sustained > 10 in launch week then silence.

## X / Twitter

- Note: no free API read path (2026) — operate via the app, treat as manual channel.
- **Threads with receipts** (screenshots, numbers, before/after) outperform opinions. Hook tweet carries everything: specific, quantified, curiosity-gapped without being bait.
- Build-in-public updates compound follower trust; pure promo doesn't.
- Reply-guys strategy for cold-start: substantive replies to large accounts in-niche put the brand in front of their audience free.

## Instagram / TikTok (faceless short-form)

- The engine renders these (carousels + UI-cuts reels); the operator's leverage is **hook selection and posting time consistency**.
- First 1.5 seconds = the scroll-stop decision: lead with the verbatim researched hook, largest text on screen.
- Carousels: one idea per slide, payoff slide before the CTA slide. Reels: captions always (sound-off viewing), loop-friendly endings.
- Hashtags: 3–5 specific > 20 generic. Bio link is the only reliable link surface — keep it pointed at the current funnel entry.
- Staging-account pattern: burn-in new formats on the low-stakes account; graduate proven formats to the flagship.

## Cold email (B2B)

Deliverability is the whole game; the copy only matters if it arrives. Send from a secondary domain (never the main), SPF+DKIM+DMARC verified, warmed 2–4 weeks, plain-text, no tracking pixels, one link max. Volume ramps 10→50/day slowly; a spam-flag rate above ~0.1% pauses everything. Sequence discipline: 3 touches max, then stop — persistence past three reads as harassment and poisons the domain.
